Services and expertise

Radiology and ultrasound in clinical context

Consultant of Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology specialising in ultrasound, 4D imaging, and paediatric, neonatal and nursery examinations.

Areas of expertise

Core clinical services

Every examination is interpreted in its clinical context, with a clear explanation that supports the patient’s ongoing care.

01

Ultrasound across specialties

The examination starts with the reason for referral and the clinical question—not an isolated image. Relevant history and previous studies are reviewed before the scan is directed toward a useful answer.

  • Assessment guided by symptoms and referral
  • Review of previous studies when relevant
  • A clear report for follow-up
02

3D and 4D ultrasound

Volumetric and real-time imaging is used when it adds clinical value, with the appropriate mode determined by the medical purpose rather than appearance alone.

  • 3D and 4D imaging where appropriate
  • Attention to image quality and documentation
  • Responsible, purpose-led use
03

Paediatric and neonatal ultrasound

Extensive experience in imaging children, newborns and patients in neonatal care, with attention to age, family reassurance and the clinical question.

  • An age-appropriate approach
  • Experience in neonatal and nursery settings
  • Simple preparation guidance for families
04

Diagnostic and interventional radiology

Consultant review that places imaging findings within the complete medical context and connects them to the appropriate diagnostic or procedural plan.

  • Consultant imaging review
  • Findings linked to the clinical question
  • Clear next-step guidance with the treating clinician
05

Mobile radiology

Smart Scan coordinates mobile radiology for patients who need the service to reach them, according to examination type, coverage and availability.

  • Initial suitability review
  • Coordination of time and location
  • Requirements explained before the visit

Before your visit

How should you prepare?

Three simple steps support a better assessment

Bring your history and previous imaging

Previous reports and images can support comparison and show how a condition has changed.

Explain the reason for the examination

The clinical question guides the scan, so bring the referring clinician’s request and any relevant information.

Confirm preparation instructions

Preparation varies by examination. Always confirm the instructions with the center before your appointment.

Important

This is general guidance. Preparation and final assessment depend on the examination, the patient’s condition and the clinical team’s instructions.
