4D ultrasound: what is the difference?

A straightforward explanation of 2D, 3D and 4D imaging and what determines the appropriate mode for an examination.

In this articleThe differenceMedical useResponsible useSources
In simple terms

2D is the conventional flat image. 3D adds a volumetric representation, while 4D displays a moving 3D view in real time.

Does a more detailed image always mean a better diagnosis?

Not necessarily. Each mode has its uses and limitations. The quality and value of the examination still depend on the clinical question, the operator’s expertise, fetal or organ position and other technical factors.

When are 3D or 4D used?

They can provide additional information in selected medical assessments, but they do not replace clinical examination or automatically replace conventional 2D ultrasound. The need and timing are determined by the medical objective.

Responsible use

Ultrasound does not use ionising radiation. Even so, specialist bodies recommend using it for a medical reason and by trained professionals, while avoiding unnecessary or prolonged exposure solely for souvenir images.

What should you ask before booking?

  • What is the medical objective of the examination?
  • Are there preparation instructions?
  • Who will perform the scan and interpret the findings?
  • How and when will the report be delivered?
The type and timing of an examination are individual medical decisions. Do not use images alone to assess pregnancy or any health condition.
