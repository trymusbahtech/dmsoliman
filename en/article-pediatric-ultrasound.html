Paediatric ultrasound: what should families expect?

A calm explanation of the steps and how you can help your child before and during the examination.

In this articleWhy it is requestedPreparationDuring the scanSources
A reassuring fact

Ultrasound uses sound waves to create images and does not rely on ionising radiation. A standard external scan is usually painless, although the child may feel light pressure from the probe or cool gel.

Why might a clinician request ultrasound?

It can assess different parts of the body depending on the child’s age, symptoms and clinical question. The most appropriate imaging method is selected for the individual case.

How can you prepare your child?

  • Explain in simple words that a small camera will move over the skin.
  • Follow fasting or water instructions carefully if provided.
  • Bring something familiar that helps your child feel calm.
  • Bring relevant previous reports and examinations.

During the examination

Gel is placed over the area and the probe is moved to obtain images. The child may need to stay still or change position. A parent’s presence depends on the centre’s policy and the nature of the examination.

Will sedation be required?

Sedation is not usually required for standard external diagnostic ultrasound. If the case has special requirements, the medical team will explain them in advance.

If your child has a specific health condition or difficulty fasting, contact the clinician or centre before the appointment rather than changing the instructions yourself.
