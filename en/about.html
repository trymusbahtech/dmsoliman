Professional experience

Dr. Mohamed Mostafa Soliman is a Consultant of Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology and a 2010 graduate of the Faculty of Medicine, Menoufia University. His work spans ultrasound and 4D imaging, with extensive experience in paediatric, neonatal and nursery examinations.

His assessment connects the referring clinician’s question, the medical history and the imaging findings to provide a clear answer in context.

Areas of expertise: ultrasound, 4D ultrasound, paediatric, neonatal and nursery ultrasound.

Alongside clinical practice, he is an accredited lecturer with the Egyptian Medical Syndicate and an accredited trainer with the Egyptian Society of Radiology.
